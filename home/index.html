Mac only. Browsers aren't allowed to open Terminal, so this downloads a tiny helper that opens it for you and runs the Homebrew install. Read it first if you like — we'd think more of you.

Where RoachNet fits

Where it actually helps.

RoachNet is for people who want their files, tools, and context to work when the internet doesn't. If you like owning your own hardware and your own data, you're in the right place.

Studio days

Docs, stems, notes, and AI in one window instead of the usual eleven-tab disaster. Still there when the studio Wi-Fi taps out mid-take.

Travel

Pack it like a suitcase. Airplane mode changes nothing.

Outages

Internet down, service down, whatever. Your files, models, maps, and vault are exactly where you left them.

Local runtime
