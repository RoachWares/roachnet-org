What's new in 0.2.0

A new look, a Home tab, and your agents on call.

0.2.0 is built for RoachNet 1.0.6 on the Mac, and ready for the agent company coming in 1.1.

The app

Made for the moments you're away from the desk.

It looks and talks like RoachNet on the Mac, so there's nothing to relearn. It opens on what you last saw and stays useful when the Mac goes quiet.

Pairs in one tap Scan the code on your Mac or open its pair link, then press Pair with RoachTail.
Useful offline Answers from what it already has when the Mac is out of reach.
Nobody in the middle Phone and Mac talk over your own network. No RoachNet server, no account.
Home tab

Brains, shelves, archives and the Mac's health on one screen.

RoachClaw Org

Approve, send back or reject with a note. Pause and resume agents. Join an Org from an invite link. (Mac 1.1 and later.)

RoachTail

Each phone gets its own key, so you can cut one off without touching the rest. Works with the 1.0.6 pairing flow.

New look

Inter and JetBrains Mono, a floating tab bar, purple for actions and green for what's live.

Steadier chat

The composer stays above the keyboard, and a paired phone never slips back into sample data.

RoachAtlas GPS

Share your location with RoachAtlas on your own Mac, only while the app is open and only when you say so.

Pairing

Pair once. The phone stays useful.

Your Mac keeps the models, the vault, and the installs. The phone stays fast because it isn't trying to be a second desktop.

Connecting

Scan the code on your Mac, or open its pair link.

RoachNet on the Mac shows a QR code and a pair link. Either one fills in the details; press Pair with RoachTail and the phone gets its own key.
